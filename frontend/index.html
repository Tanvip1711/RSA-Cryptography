RSA MATHEMATICS

How The Keys Are Generated

Follow the mathematical steps used by the C++ RSA engine to generate the key pair.

01
CHOOSE TWO PRIME NUMBERS
p = 61    q = 53

RSA starts with two different prime numbers.

↓
02
CALCULATE MODULUS
n = p × q = 3233

The modulus becomes part of both the public and private keys.

↓
03
EULER'S TOTIENT
φ(n) = (p − 1)(q − 1) = 3120

This value is used to calculate the private exponent.

↓
04
PUBLIC EXPONENT
e = 7

e is selected such that gcd(e, φ(n)) = 1.

↓
05
PRIVATE EXPONENT
d × e ≡ 1 mod φ(n)
d = 1783

d is the modular inverse of e modulo φ(n).

PRIVATE KEY (1783, 3233) Used for decryption
